# THS02


Vibes Watcher –
As the Vibes Watcher, I helped maintain a positive and supportive Team if there was work people needed help with I would make sure spirits were up and try to help as much as I could. I encouraged open communication, checked in with team members regularly and helped keep morale high during challenging parts of the project. This role was important in ensuring everyone felt comfortable contributing ideas and asking for help when needed.

I wanted to make sure that if anyone needed help they spoke up and if they were struggling that we would work together and there was no need to be down. I felt personally this role brought you a lot closer to the team and to making things work better in the long run for the remainder of the course.

Git keeper - As the Git Keeper i was responsible for managing our Git workflow. I helped organise branches for people to work out of and once a branch was complete I would merge it. sometime there was merge conflicts and I would fix them up and ensure they were working as intended and on time for the project to continue at a rate that suited the group. This helped the team collaborate efficiently and reduced the risk of losing work or introducing errors that would cost more time and issues down the line. 

I really enjoyed this as I was able to look at the work everyone had done and see how the project came together all at one point and been the git keeper I was the one ensuring the branches were done correctly

Owner –

While I was the Owner, I found this to be a experience because I had not really lead a team before. my team was very active and engaged throughout the project which made it easy to be aable to support them and help them if there was any issues that happened. I was responsible for prioritising tasks, clarifying requirements, and making sure the team stayed focused and delivered the jobs they were assigned to ensure we were progressing with the project at hand . Working with such a motivated group made it easier to coordinate progress and ensure that we met all of the project goals on time as everyone was very clear about when and where they would be available. 

Over all i did struggle slightly as i felt out of place and that i was doing something wrong. but looking back on it i am happy that i was able to step out of the comfort zone and learn something new
